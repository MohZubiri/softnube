Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services, software solutions, and delivery process.

How long does it typically take to develop a custom website or system?
Corporate landing pages and standard websites typically take 1 to 2 weeks. Custom enterprise web applications and ERP solutions take 3 to 8 weeks depending on scope, database architecture, and integration complexity.
Do you provide cloud hosting, domain setup, and ongoing maintenance?
Yes! We provide complete end-to-end turnkey packages including cloud server deployment, SSL configuration, domain registration, automated multi-destination backups, and 24/7 technical monitoring.
Can we test or demo your software (e.g. ProDentic or Shipment System) before purchasing?
Certainly. You can review the feature pages or contact our team to schedule an interactive live walkthrough demonstration tailored to your exact business workflow.
How do you guarantee data security and business continuity?
We apply strict cybersecurity best practices including data encryption at rest and in transit, role-based granular permissions, automated daily cloud backups (e.g., Google Drive and S3), and proactive uptime monitoring.
