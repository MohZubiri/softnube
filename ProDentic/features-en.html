Questions before you get started

Clear answers to the questions clinic owners ask before seeing the system.

Is it suitable for a small clinic?
Yes. The Basic option is set up for one branch, one room, and one dentist, and includes patient records, appointments, treatment plans, billing, and core daily operations.
Is the interface available in Arabic and English?
Yes. ProDentic includes a complete right-to-left Arabic interface, and users can switch to English from within the system.
Can I see the system before deciding?
Yes. Book a demo on WhatsApp and tell us your number of dentists, branches, and top priority so we can focus the walkthrough on your clinic's workflow.
How do I know which option fits?
It depends on the number of branches, rooms, dentists, and users, and whether you need reports, inventory, a waiting list, or advanced permissions. Use the form above to send the details in one message.
